FAQS

questions we get all the time • answered by the friends + family team

CAN I PICK UP AN ONLINE ORDER IN PERSON?
Some items will be available for in person pick-up – please review the item description to see if it can be picked up at our Cambridge storefront
CAN I ORDER AN ITEM TO AN ADDRESS OUTSIDE OF THE US?
No, we currently only ship domestically
CAN I GET A REFUND? CAN I EXCHANGE AN ITEM?
No, all sales are final. Please look over your purchase carefully before submitting an order or purchasing in-store
I RECEIVED MY ORDER BUT WAS SENT THE WRONG ITEM, CAN I EXCHANGE IT?
Yes, please reach out to friendsandfamllc@gmail.com and we’ll fix our mistake
I ACCIDENTALLY PUT AN INCORRECT ADDRESS FOR MY ORDER, CAN I CHANGE IT?
If this happens, reach out to friendsandfamllc@gmail.com as fast as possible and we’ll do our best to change it in time. Orders that have already shipped cannot be reshipped
WHEN DOES FRIENDS + FAMILY RESTOCK ONLINE & IN-STORE?
There isn’t a set schedule, but we try our best to have new items both online & in-store bi-weekly (if not sooner)
CAN I DONATE OR CONSIGN WITH FRIENDS + FAMILY?
No, all of our inventory comes from our network of sellers who have their own dedicated spaces within the shop. If you’re a vintage seller interested in getting involved please email friendsandfamllc@gmail.com with info about what you sell and we’ll reach out if there’s a fit
